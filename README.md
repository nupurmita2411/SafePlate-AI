# 🌿 SafePlate AI — Smart Food Safety & Healthy Lifestyle Assistant

CSE451 (Software Engineering), IUB, Autumn 2026.

## Live Website

https://safeplateai.netlify.app

## Main Features

- Dashboard & Login: User login, registration and main dashboard.
- Healthy Food Guide: Calories, protein, fibre and nutrition information.
- Kitchen Inventory: Manage available food ingredients.
- Smart Shopping List: Organize grocery items.
- AI Food Scanner: Read food package labels and identify visible ingredients.
- Food Safety Checker: Food storage rules and expiry information.
- AI Meal Planner: Suggest meals based on ingredients and preferences.
- Healthy Habit Tracker: Track water, activity and sleep.

## Technology Stack

- Frontend: HTML, CSS, JavaScript.
- Backend: Python Flask or Node.js with REST API.
- Database: SQLite or MySQL.
- Label Reading: OCR and rule-based processing.
- Meal Planning: Nutrition and ingredient-based rules.
- Hosting: Netlify.

## Simplifications (Prototype)

- Nutrition information uses public food data.
- Label reading may require user correction.
- Meal suggestions may use rule-based logic.
- Food safety cannot be guaranteed from a photo.
- Medical diagnosis and online food ordering are out of scope.
- Supabase/PostgreSQL integration is planned as future work.
